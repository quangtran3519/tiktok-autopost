Privacy Policy

TikTok Autopost. Last updated: 7 October 2026.

Who runs this tool

TikTok Autopost is a personal tool run by Quang Tran on their own computer.

What data it uses

  • From TikTok, after you sign in: your TikTok user ID, display name, username and profile picture, plus the access tokens TikTok issues so the tool can post for you.
  • The video files and caption text you place in the watched folder.

How it is used

This data is used only to upload your videos to your own TikTok inbox as drafts and to show upload status in the local dashboard. It is not used for advertising, analytics or profiling.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored only on the computer running the tool. TikTok access tokens are kept in a file readable only by the computer's user account. No data is sent to any server except TikTok's.

Sharing

Data is not sold or shared with anyone. Videos are sent only to TikTok. Captions stay on your computer until you paste them into TikTok yourself.

Deleting your data

Click Disconnect in the dashboard to delete the saved tokens, and remove the app in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions. Deleting the tool's data folder removes the upload history.
